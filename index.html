Key findings (Q1 FY26-27 and FY25-26 actuals; each one has a Q2 solution in section 5)

  1. August beat the customer-wise target. 162,710 MT vs 159,600 MT (102.0%): Enterprise 102.8%, Corporate 94.4%; Khulna Region +8.3% and Mymensingh +4.1%, but Bogura Region -8.0%.
  2. Market share is 4.82%, rank #6. The gap to #5 Premier (9.68%) is 4.86 pp, about 145,800 MT/month; the gap to leader Shah (17.89%) is 13.07 pp.
  3. FY25-26 closed at 1.929M MT, 94.9% of target. Volume grew 10.4% on FY24-25 and August 2026 grew 12.4% on August 2025.
  4. Trade and commission absorb 86.4% of marketing spend. Trade program alone is 45.2%; monthly sales commission 25.8%; brand is only 6.3%.
  5. Brand spend pays back; total spend does not. Brand ROMI is 12.6x topline / 2.3x profit on FY25-26 incremental revenue, while total marketing ROMI is -7.4% / -75.7% on Jul25-Jan26 incremental revenue.
  6. Budget discipline is uneven. Sales Incentive ran at 130.7% and Trade at 111.1% of plan, while Market Recovery (0%), PSD (6.0%) and Loyal Engineer (16.8%) were under-used.
  7. Brand/digital execution data is incomplete. ATL Q1 recorded 33% of plan; Digital has no recorded spend; a monthly tracker is needed before Q2 review.
  8. FY26-27 plan: 2.088M MT (+8.2%), BDT 22.64B revenue, BDT 1.797B marketing (43.0 BDT/bag), including BDT 207M brand (4.96 BDT/bag). Q2 needs 528,000 MT and BDT 5.72B.

What the offline data says

  • Akij is a price-led challenger. Landing rate BDT 547 is below Crown (576) and Seven Rings (551-557), while the commission package is comparable or heavier.
  • The gap to #5 is large. Premier is 4.86 pp ahead, about 145,800 MT/month. Closing it needs a step-change in dealer coverage and corporate bulk, not only a regional recovery.
  • Bogura Region is the weak spot. August was 92.0% of target; Rajshahi 90.6% and Rangpur 93.5%. Khulna (108.3%) and Mymensingh (104.1%) are over.
  • Trade spend is a retention cost. 86.4% of marketing spend is commission/trade; it protects volume but does not create incremental revenue at the current level.
